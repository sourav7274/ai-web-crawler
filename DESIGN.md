# PageBrief design
Visitor mode: Operate, transitioning to Read after submission. A user reads a summary at a desk or on a phone in ordinary ambient light; use a light background with high contrast text.
Cool paper (#f7f9fc), white result surfaces, graphite (#202a3a), muted slate (#5b6779), and cobalt (#244dd8) actions. System sans-serif avoids font downloads. The small pb. mark establishes identity without imagery.
The composition is a vertical reading flow: small masthead, clear heading, labeled URL form, summary. Avoid marketing sections and fabricated claims. Results show source, title, overview, key points.
Surface radii are 12px; buttons 7–8px. Use borders for controls and structural dividers, no decorative shadows. Desktop content is at most 860px wide; mobile controls stack. Body text has generous line height and bounded reading measures.
Visible keyboard focus and native form semantics are mandatory. Loading has a reading-line animation disabled under reduced motion. Errors explain recovery. Results render as plain text.
